# Lila

**Create space to grow.**

Lila designs thoughtful, modular, design-led furniture for kids — pieces that adapt as a child grows, moving from playtime to study and beyond without needing to be replaced.

Quality craftsmanship, safety-certified materials, and a calm, minimal aesthetic: designed for kids, styled for parents.

## What we make

- **Lila.grow** — modular pegboards with interchangeable accessories
- **Lila.stack** — modular shelving systems
- **Lila.art · Lila.rest · Lila.move · Lila.learn · Lila.play** — thematic
  collections spanning tables, chairs, beds, storage, wall accessories, climbing
  equipment, and rugs
- **Spaces by Lila** — a design consultation service for personalizing a child's room
- **Lila for Schools** — furniture and fit-outs for educational spaces
